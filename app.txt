App Name: Coloring Book
Version: 1.0.0
Platform: Android (Kotlin + Jetpack Compose)
Category: Art / Entertainment / Relaxation
Target Market: Global
Price Model: Free 20 pages + $4.99/month Pro (500+ pages)

Description:
A beautiful digital coloring book for adults and kids with hundreds of intricate designs, infinite color palette, and calming background music. Mindful coloring on your phone.

Key Features:
- 500+ coloring pages: mandalas, nature, animals, fantasy, geometric, cities
- Infinite color palette with eyedropper and custom hex input
- Multiple brush tools: fill, paintbrush, pencil, gradient fill
- Zoom in for ultra-precise coloring of fine details
- Undo/redo (unlimited)
- Save and resume unfinished colorings
- Export finished art as high-resolution PNG
- Share to Instagram, Pinterest, WhatsApp
- Calming ambient background music player
- Daily free coloring page challenge
- Time-lapse playback of your coloring session
- Kid mode: simple designs with large areas for children

Target Audience: Adults seeking mindful relaxation, art enthusiasts, parents with kids ages 5-10
Competitors: Pigment, Colorfy, Happy Color, Recolor
Unique Value Proposition: Best-in-class zoom precision + time-lapse recording of your coloring session
